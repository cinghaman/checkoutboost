# Pricing — Smart Checkout Widgets

Source: the app's billing configuration and https://smartcheckoutwidgets.com/#pricing. Last reviewed 2026-11-10, against the app's billing configuration.

Starter, Growth and Advanced include 12 widget types and every shipping and payment method rule, and differ mainly by monthly order volume. Growth and up also include customer account blocks. Pro adds a 13th widget (stock urgency), customer order editing and checkout branding. Growth, Advanced and Pro include a 7-day free trial. Yearly billing is 20% cheaper than monthly.

## Starter

- **Price:** Free. No subscription is created.
- **Order limit:** Up to 100 orders per month
- **Includes:** All widgets and all rule types

## Growth

- **Price:** $29 USD / month, or $278.40 USD / year
- **Order limit:** Up to 1,000 orders per month
- **Trial:** 7-day free trial
- **Includes:** All widgets and all rule types, plus customer account blocks (VIP status, store credit, discount codes, profile questions, return window, Get help and Reorder items)

## Advanced

- **Price:** $59 USD / month, or $566.40 USD / year
- **Order limit:** Unlimited
- **Trial:** 7-day free trial
- **Includes:** All widgets and all rule types, plus customer account blocks (VIP status, store credit, discount codes, profile questions, return window, Get help and Reorder items)

## Pro

- **Price:** $99 USD / month, or $950.40 USD / year
- **Order limit:** Unlimited
- **Trial:** 7-day free trial
- **Includes:** Everything in Advanced, plus self-serve order editing (works on every Shopify plan except Shopify Starter), low-stock badges in checkout (Shopify Plus stores) and checkout branding (Shopify Plus stores)

## Order limits

The app pauses when a store goes over its plan's monthly order limit. Widgets, gifts, upsell discounts, and shipping and payment rules all switch off. They resume automatically when the merchant upgrades or when a new month starts. Development stores are never billed or paused.

## Shopify plan requirements

Checkout UI widgets require Shopify Plus, because Shopify limits checkout customization to Plus. Thank You and Order Status page widgets, the shipping and payment method rules (Shopify Functions) and customer account blocks work on every Shopify plan. Customer account blocks need Shopify's new customer accounts.
