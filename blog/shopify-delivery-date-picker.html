Shopify delivery date picker: let customers choose a date on the product page or in the cart

Published November 25, 2026 Smart Checkout Widgets All articles

Shopify can show a customer when an order might arrive. It has no calendar where the customer picks the day. If you sell flowers, cakes, furniture or anything that needs someone at home, that choice usually ends up in the order note or in an email the next morning.

Smart Checkout Widgets now has a delivery date calendar. It works on every Shopify plan, because it lives in your theme and the checkout part uses Shopify's validation rules, which are open to all plans. The calendar itself is part of the app's paid plans.

What the customer does

They choose how they want the order (shipping, local delivery or pickup, whichever you offer) and then a day. The choice shows on the product page, on the cart page and in the cart drawer, and it stays the same in all of them. After they buy, the date can show on the Thank you and Order status pages.

You see the method and the date on the order in Shopify admin, under Additional details. With Buy it now, they are on the product line instead, because that button skips the cart.

What you decide

SettingWhat it does
Lead daysHow many days ahead the first date is. 0 allows the same day.
Cutoff timeAfter this time, one more day is added. It uses your store's time zone, wherever the customer is.
Days of the weekWhich days you deliver or open for pickup.
Closed datesHolidays and days you are fully booked.
How far aheadThe last day a customer can book.

Lead days, the cutoff and the days of the week are set separately for each way to receive an order. A shop can offer pickup tomorrow and delivery in three days.

Adding it to your theme

  1. Open Delivery Dates in the app, switch the calendar on and save.
  2. Click Switch on in theme, then save the theme.
  3. Add the Delivery date block to the Thank you and Order status pages in the checkout editor.

The calendar looks for your Check out and Add to cart buttons and goes above them. We tried it on 22 theme demo stores on desktop and phone, including Dawn, Horizon, Prestige, Impulse, Warehouse, Turbo and Empire.

If it lands in the wrong place, enter a class name or ID from your theme in the app embed settings and choose before it, after it, or inside it. That also helps with older themes. Headless storefronts won't work, since they have no theme to put it in.

Should checkout require a date?

You can leave the date optional or let checkout refuse an order without one. With the check on, checkout also refuses a date that has passed, and a mismatch such as a delivery date on an order the customer switches to pickup.

Think about these before you turn it on:

  • Every order with a physical product will need a date, including ones from draft orders, checkout links and other online sales channels. Point of sale is not checked.
  • Keep the calendar on your product pages, because Buy it now skips the cart.
  • If you offer Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal, try each button once. Shopify says the check covers express checkouts, and we have not tested those buttons ourselves yet.

If your paid plan ends, the calendar and the check switch off together, so a missing calendar can't leave customers stuck at checkout.

Watch out for Shopify's own estimates

Shopify's delivery estimates don't know which date the customer chose. In our test the customer picked 27 November while the rate at checkout said 1 to 3 December. To hide those dates, go to Settings, then Shipping and delivery, and set Delivery estimates to Off. Your rates may still show a transit time.

What it doesn't do yet

There are no time slots, daily order limits or extra charges for a date. One date covers the whole cart.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shopify have a built-in delivery date picker?

No. Shopify can show estimated delivery dates, but customers can't choose a day without an app.

Does a delivery date picker work without Shopify Plus?

Yes. The calendar runs in your theme and the checkout check is a Shopify validation rule, so both work on every Shopify plan. The feature is part of the app's Growth, Advanced and Pro plans.

Where do I see the date?

On the order in Shopify admin, under Additional details. For Buy it now orders it is on the product line.

Can I require a date before checkout?

Yes. It is off by default. When you switch it on, checkout refuses orders with a physical product that have no valid date.

Which themes does it work with?

We tried 22 theme demo stores, including Dawn and Horizon. If it lands in the wrong place, you can point it at a class name or ID in your theme. Headless storefronts are not supported.
