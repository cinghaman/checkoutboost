Which one fits

Upsell.com (formerly ReConvert): the whole path

Offers on the product page, cart, checkout and Thank you page, plus one-click funnels after payment. Free up to 50 orders or $50 of upsell revenue, then $9.99 to $39.99 as you go from 51 to 500 orders.

Aftersell: post-purchase funnels and cart

$34.99 covers one-click post-purchase, checkout and Thank you offers. The cart builder is a separate $59.99 plan, or $79.99 for both.

Zipify OCU: pay on results

Starts at $9.95 while upsell revenue is under $200 a month, moves in steps to $99 at $2,500, and continues from $149 up to a $999 ceiling.

UpsellPlus: 12 places including POS

$49 covers cart, product and post-purchase offers up to $500 of revenue. Checkout upsells and checkout blocks are on Pro at $119.

Checkout Plus: checkout blocks first

$19 covers Thank you pages, rules and post-purchase upsells. Checkout upsells, gifts at checkout and branding are on Advanced at $99, for Plus stores.

Smart Checkout Widgets: upsells as rules

You write rules such as "the cart has a snowboard, offer wax at €4 off". Rules match products, collections, tags or cart total, can stop the list, and import from a CSV. The app can suggest rules from your own orders.

You pay one price by order count, with no share of upsell revenue, and get the gifts, progress bar, rules and survey in the same app. How upsell rules work.

Not on Shopify Plus?

Skip checkout-page offers. Compare cart, product page, post-purchase and Thank you offers, since those work without Plus. A free-gift rule or a gift on the cart page can do a similar job.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add upsells to Shopify checkout without Plus?

Not on the checkout page. Shopify only opens it to apps on Plus. Cart, product page, post-purchase and Thank you page offers work on other plans.

What is the difference between checkout and post-purchase upsells?

A checkout upsell is added before payment, so it is part of the order. A post-purchase upsell appears after payment and is charged with one click.

Which upsell app takes no share of revenue?

Aftersell and Checkout Plus list plan prices, and Smart Checkout Widgets charges by store orders. Zipify, UpsellPlus and Upsell.com price by upsell revenue, orders or both.

Where does Smart Checkout Widgets show upsells?

On the checkout page for Shopify Plus stores, and on the Thank you, Order status and customer account pages on Basic and up.
