TB Bundles: build-your-own boxes, quantity breaks and mix and match for Shopify

Published October 14, 2026 Smart Checkout Widgets All articles

TB Bundles is a Shopify bundle app for five kinds of offer: fixed sets, quantity breaks, BOGO and free gifts, mix and match, and build-your-own boxes. Shoppers add the real products and variants to the cart, and the discount is applied at checkout by Shopify Functions, without a code. It's listed on the Shopify App Store as TB Bundles ‑ BYOB & Volume, with a free plan and paid plans from $12.99 a month.

TB Bundles is made by Thought Bulb, which also publishes this site. Everything below comes from the app's listing, its product page and docs.

What merchants are asking for

Recent Shopify Community threads show where Shopify's own bundles fall short. In September a merchant posted that Shopify Bundles will silently sell at the wrong price, because a fixed bundle's price doesn't follow changes to its components. In August another asked for a bundling app that ties bundle stock to the main inventory, applies bundles automatically in the cart and keeps orders editable.

The five bundle types

  • Quantity breaks. Each tier has its own minimum and discount: a percentage, an amount off or a set price. You can mark one tier "Most popular", show the per-item price, and attach a free gift to any tier.
  • Build your own box. You set the smallest and largest box, fill it from products or whole collections, and the widget shows what the next tier unlocks. A box can run on its own page without a Shopify product.
  • Mix and match across a set of products.
  • BOGO and free gifts.
  • Fixed sets of chosen products.

A "Help me choose" step asks two questions and suggests a type. "Bundle ideas" suggests products customers often buy together and turns them into a bundle in one click.

How it works in the cart

Bundles are put together from real variants when they're added to the cart, so inventory is tracked per component and there's no separate bundle product to keep in sync. Each order line gets "Part of" and "Tier" properties, so whoever packs the order can see which offer an item belongs to.

Discounts are enforced at checkout by Shopify Functions, a discount function plus a cart transform, and apply automatically. If another automatic discount gets in the way, an optional code mode creates and applies a discount code instead.

Targeting and theme

  • Show a bundle on one product or variant, a collection, a tag, or every product page, with exclusions.
  • Show it to everyone, to B2B buyers only, or to everyone except B2B buyers.
  • Include or exclude markets and countries, and set start and end dates.

It installs as an app embed with theme app blocks, so no theme files are edited. The widget matches your theme's colours by default and inherits its fonts. The docs list Horizon, Dawn and Maestrooo's Stretch, Impact and Prestige as themes tested for cart events.

Results

A dashboard shows revenue, orders and add-to-carts over 7, 30 or 90 days, compared with the previous period. The listing adds per-bundle add-to-cart clicks, orders, revenue and units sold. Shopify Sidekick can rank bundles and compare periods, but it can't edit them.

Limits

  • Subscription-only products can't go in a bundle. Products sold both ways join as one-time purchases.
  • Gift cards can't be bundled or given free, because Shopify can't discount them.
  • Draft products are blocked. Unlisted products can be sold as bundle-only items.

TB Bundles or Shopify Bundles?

Shopify's free Shopify Bundles app works on every plan and on POS, and it's enough for a fixed set or a multipack. According to Shopify's help pages it doesn't support mix and match, a bundle's price doesn't update when a component's price changes, and it can't be used with subscriptions. If you need tiers, boxes or gifts, that's where a bundle app comes in. Our bundle app comparison covers the main options side by side.

A bundle offer pairs well with a free shipping threshold: the next tier and the threshold can point at the same basket size.

Frequently asked questions

What is TB Bundles?

A Shopify bundle app for fixed sets, quantity breaks, BOGO and free gifts, mix and match, and build-your-own boxes. Discounts apply automatically at checkout through Shopify Functions.

Does TB Bundles track inventory for each item in a bundle?

Yes. Bundles are assembled from real variants when added to the cart, so stock is tracked per component.

How much does TB Bundles cost?

There's a free plan for up to $200 in lifetime bundle sales and 5 bundles. Paid plans are $12.99, $24.99 and $49.99 a month, covering $1,000, $5,000 and unlimited monthly bundle sales, each with a 14-day trial.

Can I bundle subscription products?

Not subscription-only products. Products sold both one-time and by subscription can join a bundle as one-time purchases.

Why use an app instead of Shopify Bundles?

Shopify Bundles covers fixed sets and multipacks. It doesn't support mix and match, and a fixed bundle's price doesn't update when a component's price changes.
