Six delivery date and pickup apps, what each one offers, and which shop each suits.

Which one fits

Zapiet: many locations and POS

It lists date and time slots, delivery zones and fees by postcode or distance, order limits, POS, and sending orders to DoorDash, Uber Direct or Stuart. Product preparation time and date restrictions start at $79.99, and a checkout widget is on the $179.99 plan.

Pickeasy: capacity on a small budget

Time slots with order limits, postcode validation and blackout dates are on the free plan for up to 10 orders. $9.99 adds preparation time per product and day-by-day cutoffs. The checkout page widget is an Enterprise feature at $59.99.

Stellar: unlimited orders for $14.99

One flat price for three locations, with slots, order limits, blackout dates and calendars on the product page, cart and, for Shopify Plus, checkout. Custom shipping at checkout is $20 a month extra on Plus.

DingDoong and Buunto: bakeries and florists starting out

DingDoong starts at $6.99 for 100 orders and can add a surcharge for weekend or peak-hour delivery. Buunto sets different schedules per product or collection, and adds surcharges and a postcode checker from $19.99.

Smart Checkout Widgets: a date plus the rest of checkout

Our calendar covers days, time windows, lead times, cutoffs, weekdays and closed dates, set separately for shipping, local delivery and pickup. Checkout can refuse an order without a date or time, on every Shopify plan.

The $29 plan also includes payment and shipping rules, Thank you page blocks, a post-purchase survey and customer account blocks, for stores up to 1,000 orders a month. The setup is in our delivery date guide.

Five questions before you choose

  1. Do customers need an hour, or is the day enough?
  2. Do you have to cap orders per day?
  3. Do some products need more notice than others?
  4. How many pickup locations do you have, and do their hours differ?
  5. What should happen when a customer uses Buy it now or an express button without a date? Test that in any app.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Shopify delivery date app?

It depends on what you schedule. Smart Checkout Widgets suits stores that need a day and a time window and want checkout rules, order editing and a survey in the same app. Zapiet suits many locations with POS, and Pickeasy and Stellar cover order limits per slot.

Which delivery date apps have a free plan?

Pickeasy (10 orders a month), Buunto (10), DingDoong (20) and Stellar (up to 50 orders) list one. Zapiet and Smart Checkout Widgets have a trial for this feature.

Do I need Shopify Plus for a delivery date picker?

No. Calendars on the product page and cart work on every plan. Showing a calendar on the checkout page itself needs Plus.

Can a delivery date be required at checkout?

In Smart Checkout Widgets, yes, on every Shopify plan. Check how any other app enforces it.
