Shopify split shipping: make your shipping rules work in every shipment

Published November 25, 2026 Smart Checkout Widgets All articles

Some Shopify carts now split into several shipments at checkout, each with its own list of rates. Some carts mix shipping with store pickup in one order. Rules that were written for a single list of rates can go wrong there. If a rule removes every rate from one shipment, the customer can't check out.

The one-minute video shows the checkout. The rest of this page explains what happens and how to set your rules.

Show only one rate

Take a cart with a heavy mug that ships by Freight or White glove, and a box of filters that ships by Standard or Economy. A rule says "show only Standard".

Shipment two keeps Standard and drops Economy. Shipment one has no Standard rate, so it keeps its own rates and the customer can finish. Before November 2026 the app compared the rule with all the rates at once, and shipment one could have ended up with none.

Reorder

Positions are counted from 1 inside each shipment. Move Freight and Standard to position 1, and each leads the shipment it belongs to.

Hide and rename

These match by name in every shipment, as they always did. Take care with hide rules: if a shipment has one rate and your rule hides it, that shipment has nothing left. When you mean "only offer this one", use a show-only rule, which never empties a shipment.

Ship and pickup in one order

Where Shopify lets customers ship some items and collect others, each part has its own options. A show-only rule for a shipping rate limits the shipped items and leaves the pickup alone. To be sure a rule can never touch pickup, set its Apply to scope to shipping rates.

After checkout

If customers can edit their own orders, every package is held until the edit window ends, and then they are all released. The return window on the customer's order page waits for the last package. With one package sent, it tells the customer the window starts once the order arrives.

Test yours

  1. Find two products that ship from different shipping profiles or locations.
  2. Put both in a cart and open the shipping step.
  3. Check that each shipment has at least one rate, with your rules on.

Splitting carts and mixing shipping with pickup are Shopify features, and Shopify decides which stores have them. The rules work on every Shopify plan. Order editing is part of the Pro plan, and the return window block is part of the paid plans.

Frequently asked questions

Why can a shipping rule block checkout?

If a rule removes every rate from one shipment, the customer has nothing to choose there and cannot finish. A show-only rule in Smart Checkout Widgets keeps a shipment's own rates when none of the named ones is offered.

Do shipping rules apply to each shipment separately?

Show-only and reorder rules work inside each shipment. Hide and rename rules match by name in all of them.

Can a shipping rule hide store pickup by mistake?

Set the rule's Apply to scope to shipping rates and it never touches pickup.

Do I need Shopify Plus for this?

No. Shipping method rules work on every Shopify plan.
