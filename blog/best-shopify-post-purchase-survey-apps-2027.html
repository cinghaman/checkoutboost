Best Shopify post-purchase survey apps for 2027, compared

Six ways to ask "how did you hear about us?", what each costs at 800 orders a month, and when one question is enough.

Published November 25, 2026 6 min read All articles

Short answer: choose Fairing or Zigpoll for a full attribution program with integrations, Grapevine for unlimited responses at one price, and Smart Checkout Widgets if one question with revenue per answer is enough and you want the rest of the checkout tools with it.

Which one fits

Zigpoll: surveys everywhere

Post-purchase, exit intent, email, SMS and POS, with NPS and CSAT, AI themes and syncs to Klaviyo and Google Sheets. Free for 50 responses, $39 for 200 and $129 for 600.

Grapevine: one price

$25 for unlimited responses, with targeting by order value, product, country and customer type, and data sent to Flow, Klaviyo and GA4.

Fairing: attribution for growing brands

Free up to 200 orders, then by order volume: $49, $99 at 1,000 and $149 at 5,000. It reports revenue, order value and lifetime value by channel and lists 35+ integrations.

SEA: surveys plus quizzes

$19 for 500 responses including a product recommendation quiz. Unlimited responses and A/B testing are on $49.

KNO: research teams

Templates, benchmarking and audiences. The $19 plan is one survey with three questions, and the larger plans are $119 and $299.

Smart Checkout Widgets: one question, done well

One question on the Thank you page, up to 20 answers in random order, and an optional "something else". Results shows each answer with the revenue behind it. You can reply with a one-use discount code, and see how many codes were used.

It has no follow-up questions, NPS, email or SMS surveys, or Klaviyo sync. If you need a research program, pick one of the five above. It is on every plan including the free one, and it works from Shopify Basic up.

Is one question enough?

Often, if the question is "what led you to purchase today?" and the decision is where to spend next month. One question takes a few seconds to answer. When you need to know why people bought, or how they feel a month later, use a bigger tool. Our guide to the question has wording you can copy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best post-purchase survey app for Shopify?

Fairing and Zigpoll cover the most ground, Grapevine has unlimited responses at $25, and Smart Checkout Widgets suits stores that want one question with revenue per answer.

Which post-purchase survey apps are free?

Fairing up to 200 orders, Zigpoll up to 50 responses, SEA up to 30 responses, and Smart Checkout Widgets up to 100 orders a month.

Can I give a discount for answering a survey?

Smart Checkout Widgets does, with a one-use code on every plan. The other listings we read don't describe a one-use code as a reward, so ask them.

Does a post-purchase survey need Shopify Plus?

No. Surveys on the Thank you page work without Plus. Ours needs Shopify Basic or higher.
