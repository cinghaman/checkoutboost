Which one fits

Revize: lowest flat price

Free for 20 edits a month, then $49 for unlimited edits of address, quantity and variant. Product swaps, add-ons and upsells start at $149. It lists 20+ languages and 100+ integrations.

AE: small stores that want everything

$39 covers up to 1,200 orders with every feature: adding products, recalculated tax and shipping, returns and exchanges, store credit and Shopify Flow.

ClickPost: a free start

Up to 500 orders a month it's free to install with 25 edits, then $1 an edit. $49 removes the edit limit up to 5,000 orders.

Cleverific: warehouse and ERP links

Self-serve edits, upsells with A/B testing and ERP links such as NetSuite and ShipStation. The price follows your order volume, from $59 up to 1,000 orders.

OrderEditing.com: most reviewed here

It lists shipping method and discount code changes, address checks and 100+ integrations with warehouse, fraud and subscription apps. It starts at $99.

Smart Checkout Widgets: no new payments

Customers can change the address, email, phone or note, swap to a same-price variant, lower a quantity or cancel, for 15 minutes to 24 hours. Orders can be held from fulfillment until the window closes, and a Shopify Flow trigger tells your team.

It can't add products, take an extra payment, change the country or state, or fix an order with a warehouse integration. Choose another app if you need those. The same $99 covers payment and shipping rules, a delivery date calendar, gifts and upsells in checkout on Plus, a survey and customer account blocks.

Work out your own number

  1. Count last month's "please change my order" emails.
  2. Sort them: address, size, quantity, cancel, add a product.
  3. If "add a product" is rare, you don't need to pay for it.
  4. Compare the plan for your orders in your busiest month.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best order editing app for Shopify?

For adding products and charging the difference, Revize, AE, ClickPost, Cleverific and OrderEditing.com all list it. For address, size and cancellation fixes at a flat price with checkout rules included, Smart Checkout Widgets.

Is there a free order editing app?

Revize lists a free plan with 20 edits a month, and ClickPost is free to install up to 500 orders with 25 edits.

Does Shopify let customers edit orders by itself?

Customers can ask for a cancellation or a removal where the store allows it. Changing an address, size or quantity themselves needs an app.

Do order editing apps need Shopify Plus?

No. Smart Checkout Widgets needs Basic or higher, because it runs on the Thank you and Order status pages. Check the others' listings for your plan.
