Shopify Black Friday checkout checklist for agencies and Plus teams

Build the offer early, test the complete journey, and keep launch-week changes controlled.

Smart Checkout WidgetsUpdated September 23, 2026All articles

A Black Friday checkout that holds up under load has one main promotion, discount rules someone has written down and tested, a QA pass done on real phones, and a named person who can roll things back. In 2026, Black Friday falls on November 27 and Cyber Monday on November 30. This is the order to work through it.

BFCM plan in brief

What to finish before traffic arrives

  • Choose one primary checkout objective: gift, upsell, or reassurance.
  • Configure discount combinations and inventory safeguards.
  • Test standard and accelerated checkout on real devices.
  • Freeze the build, monitor results, and document rollback steps.

1. Define the commercial rule before the widget

Write the offer down before anyone opens the app. That means the qualifying products, threshold, eligible markets, customer segments, inventory cap, start and end times, and what happens when a gift sells out. Agencies should get this one-page rule signed off by the client before building anything.

If the offer is a gift, decide whether it's added automatically or chosen by the shopper, and check the margin against a straight discount first with the free gift vs discount maths. If you're chasing order value, one checkout upsell that fits the basket does more than a carousel of unrelated products.

2. Verify discount combinations

Shopify only combines two discounts when each one is set to allow it. When they can't combine, Shopify applies whichever gives the customer the best result, which may not be the offer you advertised. There are hard limits too: at most 25 active automatic discounts per store, app discounts included, and at most five product or order codes plus one shipping code on a single order, according to Shopify's discount combinations guide. Test the exact combinations you advertise in email, ads and onsite banners. If codes are already refusing to stack, Thought Bulb's guide to Shopify discount codes not stacking on Black Friday covers the usual causes.

3. Design the checkout as a sequence

  1. Confirm the gift or deal the shopper has earned.
  2. Show one relevant add-on, as long as it doesn't compete with that reward.
  3. Answer delivery, returns, warranty or payment questions next to the step where they come up.

Smart Checkout Widgets handles gifts, upsells and trust content in one app. Those appear through checkout UI extensions, which Shopify only allows in the information, shipping and payment steps on Shopify Plus. Its payment and shipping method rules run as Shopify Functions and work on every plan. The guide to what belongs in checkout on Black Friday covers what to put in each slot.

4. Run a production-minded QA pass

  • Test below, at, and above every spend threshold.
  • Test signed-in, guest, and Shop Pay or other accelerated checkout paths.
  • Check narrow mobile screens, keyboard navigation, and long translations.
  • Verify sold-out gifts, removed qualifying items, refunds, and edited orders.
  • Confirm event tracking and campaign naming before launch.

Shopify's own BFCM preparation guide also covers store policies, shipping, checkout recovery, reporting and order operations, which sit outside the checkout itself. For problems that tend to show up during the weekend, Thought Bulb keeps a list of Shopify Black Friday problems and fixes.

5. Freeze, monitor, and roll back

Finish full QA at least a week before launch. During the event, one person approves changes and another watches errors, gift stock, discount usage and what customers are contacting support about. Decide the fallback in advance: switch off the optional upsell first, then simplify the offer. If you edit several rules at once under pressure, you won't know which change broke something.

Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Cyber Monday follows on November 30, 2026.

When should a Shopify store finish BFCM checkout QA?

Finish the build and QA on real devices at least a week before launch. After that, only approved fixes and content updates go live.

What should a Shopify Plus team test before Black Friday?

Test gift eligibility, discount combinations, upsell relevance, payment presentation, mobile layout, accelerated checkout, translations, inventory limits, and analytics.
