Shopify Black Friday checkout checklist for agencies and Plus teams

Build the offer early, test the complete journey, and keep launch-week changes controlled.

Smart Checkout WidgetsUpdated August 28, 2026All articles

The short answer: a reliable Black Friday checkout plan has one primary promotion, explicit discount rules, a mobile-first QA pass, and a rollback owner. In 2026, Black Friday falls on November 27 and Cyber Monday on November 30.

BFCM plan in brief

What to finish before traffic arrives

  • Choose one primary checkout objective: gift, upsell, or reassurance.
  • Configure discount combinations and inventory safeguards.
  • Test standard and accelerated checkout on real devices.
  • Freeze the build, monitor results, and document rollback steps.

1. Define the commercial rule before the widget

Start with the outcome and constraints. Write down the qualifying products, threshold, eligible markets, customer segments, inventory cap, start and end times, and what happens when a gift sells out. Agencies should get this one-page rule approved before implementation.

For a gift-led campaign, decide whether the reward is added automatically or selected by the shopper. For a revenue-led campaign, use one high-relevance checkout upsell rather than a carousel of unrelated products.

2. Verify discount combinations

Do not assume every promotion stacks. Shopify requires merchants to configure discount combinations, and incompatible discounts can change which offer applies. Shopify also documents limits for active automatic discounts and codes used in one order. Review the current Shopify discount combinations guidance, then test the exact combinations advertised in email, paid media, and onsite banners.

3. Design the checkout as a sequence

  1. Reward: confirm the earned gift or deal.
  2. Expansion: show one relevant add-on if it does not compete with the reward.
  3. Reassurance: answer delivery, returns, warranty, or payment questions near the decision.

Smart Checkout Widgets brings gifting, upsells, trust content, and payment presentation into one campaign system for Shopify Plus checkout. Checkout UI extensions on the information, shipping, and payment steps are a Shopify Plus capability.

4. Run a production-minded QA pass

  • Test below, at, and above every spend threshold.
  • Test signed-in, guest, and Shop Pay or other accelerated checkout paths.
  • Check narrow mobile screens, keyboard navigation, and long translations.
  • Verify sold-out gifts, removed qualifying items, refunds, and edited orders.
  • Confirm event tracking and campaign naming before launch.

Shopify’s own BFCM preparation guide also recommends checking store policies, shipping, checkout recovery, reporting, and order operations. Checkout merchandising is one part of the complete operating plan.

5. Freeze, monitor, and roll back

Complete full QA at least a week before launch. During the event, assign one person to approve changes and one to watch errors, gift inventory, discount usage, and support themes. Keep a tested fallback ready: disable the optional upsell first, then simplify the offer rather than editing several rules under pressure.

Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Cyber Monday follows on November 30, 2026.

When should a Shopify store finish BFCM checkout QA?

Finish the main build and full-device QA at least one week before launch, then limit changes to approved fixes and content updates.

What should a Shopify Plus team test before Black Friday?

Test gift eligibility, discount combinations, upsell relevance, payment presentation, mobile layout, accelerated checkout, translations, inventory limits, and analytics.
