Add a "How did you hear about us?" survey to your Shopify Thank you page

Published November 18, 2026 Smart Checkout Widgets All articles

Over Black Friday, Meta and Google can each count the same order as a conversion from their ads, and your Shopify and GA4 reports may credit a third channel. The buyer can tell you where they first heard of you, which none of those reports can. Ask them on the Thank you page, with one question, and put their answers next to the reports.

The short answer

Create a Post-purchase survey in Smart Checkout Widgets, change the question to "How did you first hear about us?", and edit the answers to match the channels you actually use. Then add the Post-purchase survey block to the Thank you page in Shopify's checkout editor. The app's Results page shows each answer's share and the revenue of the orders behind it for the last 7, 30 or 90 days.

Why ask on the Thank you page and not by email

Every buyer sees the Thank you page right after paying. The purchase is a minute old, so it's the easiest moment to remember where they saw you. A survey email arrives later, lands next to the order confirmation and shipping updates, and only counts the people who open it.

The answer is also tied to the order. Results shows the revenue behind each answer, which an email reply days later can't give you unless you match replies to orders by hand.

Word the question for new buyers

The default question is What led you to purchase today?. For holiday acquisition I'd change it to How did you first hear about us?. The word "first" does the work. Without it, a loyal customer who clicked your Black Friday email picks Email, and you already knew your email list sells. With it, they tell you how they found you in the first place, or they pick "I already knew the brand".

The default answers are a reasonable start:

  • An AI assistant (e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude)
  • Google
  • Instagram
  • TikTok
  • Facebook
  • Recommended by a friend
  • Recommended by an expert or professional
  • A link from a website or blog
  • Email
  • I already knew the brand

Edit the list so it matches what you spent money on this season. If you sponsored a podcast, add "A podcast". If YouTube was a real channel for you, give it its own line. You can have up to 20 answers, one per line, but on a phone a list of around ten is already long.

Keep the AI assistant answer even if you don't market there. Shoppers who ask ChatGPT for a recommendation and then type your address show up as direct traffic, and this answer is the only place you'll see them. The AI assistant attribution guide covers that channel in detail.

Leave shuffling on, so the first answer in your list doesn't win because of where it sits. Turn on "Something else" so buyers can type a source you didn't list. It always shows last.

First-time and returning buyers

The survey shows to every buyer who reaches the Thank you page. Its settings don't include an option to show it only on a customer's first order, so returning customers answer too. The wording above handles most of this. "First" points returning buyers back to how they originally found you, and "I already knew the brand" gives them an honest answer that doesn't inflate your paid channels.

Reading the results next to Shopify and GA4

Open Results in the app and pick 7, 30 or 90 days. For each answer you see how many buyers picked it, its share of all answers, the revenue of those orders and its share of that revenue. Below that are the latest typed-in "Something else" answers. Results counts from the first order the app recorded after install, so set the survey up before the sale starts if you want the whole of Black Friday in it.

Then line the 30-day shares and the revenue behind them up against the same dates in Shopify's marketing reports and in GA4. The useful part is where they disagree. If a lot of buyers say a friend recommended you while analytics shows mostly direct visits, word of mouth is doing more than any report credits. If Meta reports far more conversions than buyers credit to Instagram and Facebook combined, some of those orders probably started somewhere else.

What self-reported answers can't tell you

An answer is what the buyer remembers. Someone who saw a TikTok, read a review and then searched your name can honestly pick any of the three, and someone whose real source isn't listed picks the closest one. Answering is also optional, so the shares are shares of buyers who answered, which is a smaller group than all your orders.

Small numbers swing too. On a few dozen answers, two or three orders move a share a long way. Use a week of Black Friday answers as a first look, and trust a trend over 30 or 90 days more. Changing the answers mid season makes the before and after hard to compare, so settle the list before the sale and leave it until January.

Set it up before the sale

  1. In Smart Checkout Widgets, open Widgets and create a Post-purchase survey. Change the question, edit the answers, choose whether to shuffle them, name the "Something else" answer and set the thank-you line buyers see after answering. Save.
  2. In Shopify, go to Settings > Checkout, open the checkout editor for your configuration and switch to the Thank you page.
  3. Click Add block, choose Post-purchase survey and save. There's no widget ID to paste.

Answers you give while trying it in the checkout editor preview are never saved, so testing won't skew your numbers. One answer is kept per checkout, so no order is counted twice. The survey docs and the Results docs have the details.

Which plans it works on

On the Shopify side the survey needs the Basic plan or higher. Shopify's Starter plan can't run Thank you page blocks. On the app side it's included on every Smart Checkout Widgets plan. Check your order limit before Black Friday, though. Starter covers up to 100 orders a month and Growth up to 1,000, and when a store goes over its limit the app pauses its widgets until you upgrade or the next month starts. That would switch the survey off in the week you most want answers. Advanced and Pro have no order limit.

If you're still deciding what else goes on the page, see what to show on the Thank you and Order status pages and the Black Friday checkout checklist.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a "How did you hear about us?" survey to Shopify?

Create a Post-purchase survey in Smart Checkout Widgets and change the question to "How did you hear about us?". Then open the Thank you page in Shopify's checkout editor, click Add block, choose Post-purchase survey and save. There is no widget ID to paste.

Does a post-purchase survey need Shopify Plus?

No. Shopify allows app blocks on the Thank you and Order status pages from the Basic plan up, and only the checkout page itself needs Plus. The survey is included on every Smart Checkout Widgets plan, including the free Starter plan.

Should I ask on the Thank you page or in a follow-up email?

The Thank you page. Every buyer sees it right after paying, while they still remember how they found you, and each answer is tied to the order, so you can see the revenue behind it. An email survey only reaches buyers who open it, days later.

Can I show the survey only to first-time customers?

The survey settings don't include a first-order-only option, so returning customers see it too. Ask "How did you first hear about us?" and keep the "I already knew the brand" answer, so returning buyers have an honest choice that doesn't count toward your paid channels.

How accurate is post-purchase attribution?

It is what buyers remember. People who saw you in several places pick one, and people whose real answer isn't listed pick the nearest one. Use the shares to spot gaps in your analytics and to watch trends over 30 or 90 days, and don't treat them as exact numbers.
