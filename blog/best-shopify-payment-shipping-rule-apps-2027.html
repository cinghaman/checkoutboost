Which one fits

HidePay and HideShip: a pair

One for payments, one for shipping. Rules with several conditions start at $5.99. HidePay lists hiding express buttons, and HideShip lists importing postcodes by CSV and creating shipping rates.

Payfy and PayRules: payment rules only

Payfy lists hiding COD for PO boxes and postcodes, B2B conditions and metafields on $7.99. PayRules counts active rules: 5, 10 or 25.

Kip: $3

Unlimited rules to hide, rename and sort payment methods, and hiding express checkout buttons. One hide rule is free.

Blockify: rules and validation

Adds checkout validation, order limits, COD fees and discounts. It's the choice if you need to stop an order, which ours doesn't do.

Smart Checkout Widgets: both, with no limit by plan

Hide, rename and reorder payment and shipping methods, or show only the shipping rates you name. Conditions include cart total, product tag, collection, customer tag, country, postcode ranges, weekday and date. Rules work inside each shipment when Shopify splits a cart.

It can't hide express wallets such as Shop Pay, add a COD fee, use the time of day, or block an order for anything except a missing delivery date. Customer conditions only match signed-in buyers.

Test any of them in five minutes

  1. Write the rule and note exactly which method name it matches.
  2. Check out as a guest with an address that should trigger it.
  3. Check out with one that shouldn't.
  4. Try express buttons and a cart that splits into two shipments.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Shopify Plus to hide payment methods?

No. Apps that use Shopify Functions hide, rename and sort payment and shipping methods on every Shopify plan.

What is the cheapest app to hide Cash on Delivery?

Kip lists one hide rule free and unlimited rules for $3 a month. Smart Checkout Widgets is free up to 100 orders a month with no rule limit by plan.

Can one app handle both payment and shipping rules?

Blockify and Smart Checkout Widgets list both in one app. HidePay and HideShip are two apps with a bundle price.

Can an app hide the Pick up option?

No. Shopify lets apps hide single pickup locations, but not the Pick up method itself.
