◇ faq

Questions merchants ask before installing.

Does Smart Checkout Widgets require Shopify Plus?

No. Payment and shipping method rules and Thank You and Order Status page widgets work on every Shopify plan. Widgets on the checkout page itself (gifts, upsells, custom fields, trust badges in checkout) need Shopify Plus, because Shopify only allows checkout-page customization on Plus.

Is there a free plan or trial?

Starter is free to install and covers up to 100 orders a month with every widget and rule type included. Growth and Advanced come with a 7-day free trial.

What is the difference between the plans?

Every plan includes all 12 widgets and all shipping and payment rules. Plans differ only by monthly order volume: Starter up to 100, Growth up to 1,000, Advanced unlimited. Yearly billing saves 20%.

How do automatic gifts work in Shopify checkout?

You set a rule such as a spend threshold or specific products in the cart. When the cart meets it, the app adds the gift product at checkout and discounts it to free. No code is needed. Gifts can also be tied to a discount code, and you can let the customer choose from several gifts with a cap on how many they take.

Can I hide, rename or reorder shipping and payment methods?

Yes. Rules run as Shopify Functions and can hide, rename or re-order delivery options and payment methods based on cart total, quantity, weight, customer tag, order count, product tag, collection, country, province, city, postcode, day of week or date. Shipping rules can be scoped to shipping rates, local delivery, carrier pickup points, local pickup locations or retail.

Can I hide the Pick up option entirely?

No app can. Shopify allows hiding individual pickup locations but not the Pick up delivery method itself.

What happens when I go over my plan's order limit?

Widgets pause once a store passes 25% over its monthly limit, not the moment it is reached. Upgrading re-enables them automatically, and they also switch back on when a new month starts.

Does it work with checkout.liquid?

No, and it does not need to. The app uses Shopify checkout extensibility, the supported replacement for checkout.liquid, so nothing breaks when Shopify updates checkout.
